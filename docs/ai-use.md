# Disclosure of AI-assisted development

`ordfisher` was developed with assistance from OpenAI ChatGPT. During the November 2026 revision and release preparation, the GPT-6 model (OpenAI) was used to help draft and review Python source code, propose and refine automated tests, prepare scripts for reproducing mathematical and simulation results, and improve the documentation and scientific writing.

The use of ChatGPT served to accelerate development and support debugging and cross-checking; it does **not** constitute independent scientific evidence. Scientific authorship and responsibility remain with Giovanni Campanini, Fernando Pose, Carlos Garcia, and Francisco O. Redelico, who are responsible for reviewing the code, analytical derivations, tests, input data, scientific interpretations, and final release.

## Reproducibility and provenance

- OPS and OFS are evaluated by numerical linear algebra with explicit mathematical definitions, not by obtaining numerical claims directly from language-model responses.
- The stationary Markov certificate was newly reconstructed from exact rational constraints. It is not the unrecovered historical certificate file used in earlier research iterations.
- Seeded simulations use documented, newly generated sequences; they need not match the historical realizations pixel for pixel.
- The version-specific DOI and the public repository URL must not be filled in before a real deposit and publication.
- The authors should confirm the model versions used in **earlier** ChatGPT sessions if the publisher requests a complete historical tool-version inventory. The model identified here (GPT-6) reflects the November 2026 release-preparation stage.
